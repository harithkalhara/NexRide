# Design references — reviewed 14 October 2026

The visual design and copy are original to this release. Reference sites informed workflow decisions; their logos, UI source, customer reviews and business statistics were not copied.

| Reference | Observed pattern | NexRide implementation |
|---|---|---|
| https://www.eaglerider.com/ | Rental location and availability search near the top of the page | City, bike type and date search on homepage and catalogue |
| https://www.riders-share.com/how-it-works/ | Clear progression from finding a vehicle through pickup and return | Choose / request / pay & pick up explanation; dashboard tracks later return |
| https://www.donkey.bike/pricing | Bicycle pricing explained around the rental period | Daily prices, bicycle category, inclusive-day estimate and visible long-rental discount |

Visual direction: warm ivory, dark navy, burnt orange; original N road-mark logo; large editorial headings; asymmetrical photo-led hero; spacious product cards; compact operational tables; mobile layouts and reduced-motion styles.

No invented customer endorsements, transaction counts, safety guarantees or insurance claims appear in the interface. Stock images remain explicitly labelled where they illustrate a specific vehicle listing.
